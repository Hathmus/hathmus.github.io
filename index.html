method

Everything here is triple-sourced, and the sources are published alongside the film.

Claims are traced to primary material wherever primary material survives. Translations are made from the original language and marked as translations. Where a source is a summary rather than a direct quotation, the narration says so out loud.

Corrections are kept, not quietly removed. If a map is misattributed, a name collides with another name, or a date is wrong, that correction stays on the record — because how the error happened is usually part of the story.
