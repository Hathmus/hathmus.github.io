Three Men Who Fixed the Record

A chronologist, a physician and a librarian. Between them they decided what year it was, where north was, and which old documents were real.

Throw a dart at a man. Each hit tells you more about him. Four hits and he has nothing left to give.

the board

Every panel is marked for what it is.

Where a line is documented, it says so, and the source is linked. Where the reading is mine, it says that too, in a different colour, and names which part of the panel is argument rather than record. Nothing here asks you to take the connection on trust — the dates and the quotations stand on their own, and you can disagree with what I make of them.

This page is an experiment. If it works it will grow.
